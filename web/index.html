QUICK GUIDE

Read. Ask. Keep.

  1. Open a notebook and add a PDF.
  2. Select text, then choose Highlight & save to keep it in Notes.
  3. The notes icon at the right saves the current page, including a snapshot. The lightbulb explains that page.
  4. Use the chat button at the bottom right to ask about the page in view. Choose Chart, Visual, Simplify, or Detailed.

Questions keeps your question history. Notes lets you read and edit what you’ve saved.
